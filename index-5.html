Forward-Looking Allocation in Ukraine's e-Points System

This independent concept note examines whether a controlled lending-pool mechanism could help Ukrainian units better align e-Points access with forward-looking operational need. The proposal is based only on public information and does not include tactical, locational, or unit-sensitive details.

I am sharing this work selectively for feedback from Ukrainian practitioners, defense-innovation specialists, and researchers familiar with drone procurement and battlefield logistics.

This project is independent and does not represent Princeton University, the Government of Ukraine, Brave1, Signum, or any military organization.

For access to the concept note or full technical memo, please reach out directly. I welcome corrections, challenge, and perspective from those with operational knowledge I lack.
